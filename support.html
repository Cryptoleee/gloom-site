An effect shows a ring where the shutter is

That effect belongs to a drop you have not unlocked. You can try it in the camera; to keep what you shoot, tap the ring and unlock the drop, or everything at once.

I bought a drop and it is locked again

Tap a ring, then Restore purchases at the bottom of the sheet. Use the Apple account you bought it with.

Where are my clips

Tap the square in the bottom left corner of the camera. Clips and photos are also saved to your photo library if you allowed that.

How do I zoom

Push the lever on the right edge of the picture up or down. Flick it for a crash zoom.
