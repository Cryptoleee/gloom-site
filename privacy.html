Gloom

Privacy.

What Gloom collects

Nothing. Gloom has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not track you.

Camera and microphone

Gloom uses the camera to draw its picture and the microphone to record sound with your clips. Everything is processed on your iPhone. Nothing you film leaves the phone unless you share it yourself.

Your clips and photos

What you shoot is kept inside the app and, if you allow it, saved to your photo library.

When you import a photo or video, you pick it yourself in Apple's picker. Gloom only gets the items you pick, and only on your iPhone.

When you turn a photo in Gloom that you saved earlier, Gloom asks for access to your library so it can turn the copy there too. It only touches photos it made itself.

Purchases

Drops are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Gloom only learns which drops are unlocked.
